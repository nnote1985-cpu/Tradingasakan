# v1.1 plan: trend_donchian_4h_v1.1 (pre-declared)

Written 2026-10-15, **before any v1.1 backtest was run**. Owner decision: fix
v1 before building the AI filter (Phase 0.5 verdict `REVISE_HYPOTHESIS`).

## Why v1 failed (Phase 0.5, see `phase-0.5-spike.md`)

- Development trades lost -0.09R each **before** costs; validation made +0.07R
  before costs but paid ~0.08R in fees and funding.
- 1h breakouts with a 2R target and a 24h time exit cap the winners, so fees
  are a large share of each trade's expected move.
- About 13 trades/month on one symbol is too few to measure an AI filter.

## Hypothesis

Trend following works on crypto at longer horizons, where winners run for
days or weeks and a few large trends pay for many small losses. Moving the
signal to 4h bars, removing the fixed take profit and adding symbols should
(1) raise gross expectancy per trade, (2) shrink fees as a share of R, and
(3) give more trades per month in total.

These changes come from the standard Donchian/turtle trend-following design,
not from searching v1's results. v1's development and validation results have
already been seen, so the **holdout (2025-01-25 to 2025-12-31) is the only
clean test** and stays untouched until v1.1 is frozen.

## v1.1 rules (defaults, fixed now)

Signal on each closed, valid 4h bar (UTC 00/04/08/12/16/20):

- LONG: EMA50(4h) > EMA200(4h), close4h > EMA200(4h), close4h > highest high
  of the previous 20 4h bars.
- SHORT: mirror image.
- Entry: first 1m bar opening at or after the 4h close (market order, taker).
- Initial stop: 2 × ATR14(4h) from the entry estimate. Same sizing as v1
  (0.5% of equity, cap 50 USDT, same allowances).
- No take profit. Exit at the next bar open after a 4h close below the lowest
  low of the previous 10 4h bars (LONG; mirror for SHORT), after a 4h close
  across EMA200 (same trend exit as v1), or at the stop. Safety time exit 60 days.
- Cooldown 4h after an exit.
- Symbols: BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, each run as its own 10,000 USDT book;
  the portfolio is the sum of the three books (30,000 USDT).
- Benchmark: equal-weight buy & hold of the same three symbols (and BTC alone).
- Instrument filters (APPROXIMATE, to verify): BTC tick 0.1 / step 0.001,
  ETH tick 0.01 / step 0.001, SOL tick 0.01 / step 1. Min notional 100.

## Pre-declared runs

1. v1 and v1.1 on all three symbols, development and validation,
   conservative fills, halt `absorbing` and `record_only`.
2. Cost grid for v1.1: fee multiplier {0.4, 1, 2} × slippage multiplier {0, 1, 2, 4}.
   Fee 0.4 approximates maker fees on entries and is reported, not assumed.
3. Sensitivity on development only, one axis at a time, diagnostics only:
   entry lookback {20, 55}, exit lookback {10, 20}, stop ATR multiple {2, 3}.
   Nothing is selected from this grid; the defaults above stay the candidate.

## Success criteria (fixed now; "beat the market" not yet confirmed by owner)

v1.1 is "fixed enough to continue" if, at default costs with `record_only`:

1. Portfolio net return > 0 on development **and** on validation, and
2. Net expectancy per trade > 0 on both partitions, and
3. On validation, portfolio Sharpe ≥ equal-weight buy & hold Sharpe **or**
   max drawdown ≤ half of buy & hold's with positive return.

Otherwise the result is reported as is and the next hypothesis is chosen
with the owner.
